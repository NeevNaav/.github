Company Overview
NeevNaav is a cybersecurity, AI governance, and digital resilience company focused on building AI‑native, security‑by‑design, and resilience‑by‑default solutions for enterprises and MSMEs. The company operates as a strategic partner to boards, CXOs, and technology leaders, helping them design, implement, and continuously improve cyber resilience, data protection, and AI governance programs.

Mission
NeevNaav’s mission is to enable organizations to move from reactive security to predictive, intelligence‑driven, and self‑adaptive digital trust systems. The company aims to simplify complex cybersecurity and compliance challenges through practical architectures, automation, and AI‑driven insights, especially for high‑growth and resource‑constrained organizations.

Vision
NeevNaav envisions a world where enterprises, governments, and MSMEs can confidently adopt AI and digital transformation while maintaining strong security, privacy, and compliance. The company seeks to become a trusted global partner for AI‑native cyber resilience, digital sovereignty, and responsible AI governance.

Core Offerings

4.1 Virtual CISO and Data Protection Office

Fractional and virtual CISO services for organizations that require strategic security leadership without a full‑time CISO.

Design and implementation of security programs aligned with business goals, risk appetite, and regulatory requirements.

Establishment of security governance structures, policies, and processes across the organization.

4.2 Cyber Resilience and Incident Readiness

Development of cyber resilience frameworks that integrate prevention, detection, response, and recovery.

Enterprise‑wide threat modeling aligned with frameworks such as MITRE ATT&CK and Cyber Kill Chain.

Data Security Posture Management (DSPM) strategies and controls across multi‑cloud and hybrid environments.

4.5 AI Governance and Responsible AI

Design and implementation of AI governance models aligned with ISO 42001 and global AI regulatory trends.

Development of AI risk management frameworks, AI system inventories, and impact assessments.

Integration of AI governance with cybersecurity, data protection, and enterprise risk management.

4.6 MSME‑Focused Security and Compliance

Tailored cybersecurity and privacy programs for MSMEs with limited resources but high risk exposure.

Lightweight assessment and automation toolkits to help MSMEs rapidly achieve security maturity and compliance readiness.

Sector‑specific implementation playbooks for industries such as fintech, healthcare, manufacturing, and professional services.

Products and Platforms

5.1 NIRMETRA
NIRMETRA is conceived as an AI‑native cyber resilience platform that brings together threat intelligence, risk scoring, and automated response. It supports the design of autonomous SOC concepts, focusing on early detection, predictive analytics, and resilience metrics.

5.2 Niriksha AI
Niriksha AI is focused on AI‑native observability and monitoring, integrating logs, metrics, traces, and security events to provide real‑time situational awareness and root cause analysis. It is designed to help organizations understand the behavior of complex digital systems and respond faster to anomalies and threats.

5.3 IndiGinius
IndiGinius represents a set of frameworks and tools to support digital sovereignty, trusted AI, and national or sectoral cyber resilience. It emphasizes India‑first and globally relevant architectures for critical infrastructure, digital public goods, and sovereign digital ecosystems.

5.4 CyberCapsule
CyberCapsule is a concept and platform for packaging cyber resilience, AI governance, and compliance capabilities into modular, reusable building blocks. It targets organizations that need structured yet flexible capabilities to adopt secure digital transformation.
